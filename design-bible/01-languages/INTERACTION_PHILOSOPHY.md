## §1 · The one interaction sentence
**The visitor performs the Maker's capabilities; they do not read them.** Interaction turns a
viewer into a discoverer and a discoverer into a believer.

## §2 · The Verb principle (core)
- Key capability moments are expressed as **one small action the visitor performs** — the verb
  *is* the capability. (Forest canon verbs: REVEAL, DIRECT, CONNECT, SEE, KINDLE.)
- A capability shown by a *verb* outranks a capability shown by a *label* every time.
- New interactive moments should be expressible as a single clear verb. If you can't name the
  verb, the interaction isn't designed yet.

## §3 · Dormant → Alive (the core loop)
- Interactive places begin **inert** and the visitor's act **brings them to life.** The recurring
  "I made this come alive" feeling is the World's signature loop, and it quietly casts the
  visitor as the Maker (self-portrait).
- Reward must be immediate, generous, and *visible* — a transformation, a bloom, a machine
  springing to life, light spreading.

## §4 · Trivial, never frustrating (the difficulty law)
- Interactions are **near-obvious**: one step, one click, one movement. **This is a portfolio,
  not a puzzle game.**
- **No fail states. No friction. No challenge.** The *reward*, not the difficulty, is the point.
- A recruiter must never be confused about *how* to interact or be made to feel stupid. Any
  interaction that can frustrate a time-poor visitor is rejected.

## §5 · The Responsive World (baseline)
- The environment **reacts to presence** throughout — light turns, paths brighten, things stir
  as the visitor approaches. This is continuous proof that "the Maker builds experiences that
  consider the human."
- Responses are **subtle and ambient** by default; full verb-interactions are the special beats.

## §6 · Diegetic over UI
- Express through the **world** (a lit lantern, a gate marker, a blooming sketch) before reaching
  for screen UI.
- **Overlay/UI is a fallback**, used sparingly: depth-on-approach text (proximity panels), the
  Fast Path control, and essential controls. UI is never the primary way meaning is delivered.
- UI that does appear must respect the humane tone (warm, restrained) — no game-HUD clutter.

## §7 · The Fast Path (canon)
- A **persistent, subtle affordance** lets a time-poor visitor jump straight to the proof
  (Projects) without traversing the World.
- It must always be reachable, never intrusive. The narrative stays linear; **access is
  non-linear** (NARRATIVE_SPINE §6). Trapping a hurried recruiter is forbidden.

## §8 · Motion & camera feel (principles, not implementation)
- **Smooth, frame-rate-independent, weighted.** Motion (camera, float, bob, transitions) feels
  cinematic and consistent regardless of device — never jittery, never frame-rate-dependent.
- **Gentle and subtle** over flashy: a slow float, an easing transition, a soft bob. Animation
  serves mood and life, not spectacle.
- **Readable pacing:** the visitor is never rushed by the camera or disoriented by motion.
- Player movement is consistent and predictable; controls are immediately intuitive.

## §9 · Accessibility & respect for the visitor
- Reachable by the curious *and* the rushed (Fast Path).
- No reliance on twitch skill, precise timing, or hidden mechanics to understand the Maker.
- Clear, forgiving, generous. The World flatters the visitor; it never tests them.

## §10 · What BELONGS / what does NOT
**Belongs:** a single clear verb that performs a capability; dormant→alive reveals; subtle
presence-reactions; smooth weighted camera/float; generous immediate reward; a quiet ever-present
Fast Path; restrained diegetic feedback.

**Does NOT belong:** puzzles, fail states, timers, friction, or difficulty; unclear "how do I
interact?" moments; flashy spectacle animation; jittery/frame-rate-dependent motion; game-HUD
clutter; UI as the primary meaning-carrier; forced linear traversal with no skip; any
interaction that can frustrate a time-poor recruiter.

## §11 · Evaluation checklist (for agents — "does this interaction belong?")
1. **Verb** — is there one clear action, and does it *perform* a capability? (§2)
2. **Dormant→alive** — does the act bring something to life with a generous reward? (§3)
3. **Trivial** — near-obvious, no fail state, no friction, can't frustrate? (§4)
4. **Responsive baseline** — consistent with a world that reacts to presence? (§5)
5. **Diegetic-first** — expressed in-world, not via primary UI? (§6)
6. **Fast Path respected** — does not trap the hurried; skip remains available? (§7)
7. **Motion feel** — smooth, weighted, frame-rate-independent, subtle not flashy? (§8)
8. **Respect** — clear, forgiving, no twitch/timing/hidden mechanics? (§9)
