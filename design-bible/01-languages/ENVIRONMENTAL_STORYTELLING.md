## §1 · The one storytelling sentence
**The space tells the story; words are the last resort.** The visitor should *understand the
Maker by walking*, not by reading.

## §2 · Show, don't tell (the primary medium)
- Meaning is delivered by **composition, state, light, and arrangement** — never by a label as
  the first choice. A wooden sign explaining a skill is a failure of environmental storytelling.
- **The sole canonical exception is the Verification Zone (Mountains):** facts (company, role,
  duration) must be *legible* there. Everywhere else, show-don't-tell is law.
- If a meaning can only be conveyed with text, the environment hasn't been designed yet — keep
  designing until the *space* says it.

## §3 · The Absent Maker (the conceit)
- The Maker is **felt everywhere, seen nowhere.** No literal avatar of the Maker is shown.
- Presence is conveyed through **traces**: a lit lantern, a tended path, tools mid-use, a warm
  fire, a half-finished sketch. The World is his, and he has *just stepped away.*
- **Freshly inhabited, never abandoned.** Signs of *recent, living* presence — warmth, light,
  motion. Never ruin, never melancholy, never decay-for-its-own-sake.

## §4 · The Self-Portrait (the frame)
- The World is the Maker's mind/identity, walked by the visitor; the quiet realisation that
  "this is a person, and I've been inside their head" is the payoff.
- The dormant→alive loop reinforces this: animating the World casts the visitor *as* the Maker.
- *(LOCKED for the Forest; whole-World scope is UNRESOLVED — do not assume it beyond the Forest
  without the Maker's decision.)*

## §5 · Every element earns its meaning
- Every placed element should carry **meaning, navigation, or composition** value (Bible §12).
  "It's a nice prop" is not a reason. Filler dilutes the story.
- **Redundancy beats proliferation.** A *few* core ideas reinforced by *multiple* elements are
  perceived; *many* thin one-off meanings are not. (Memory over completeness.) Express each core
  idea through 2–3 reinforcing elements rather than twenty private symbols.

## §6 · Evidence anchors (keeping magic professional)
- Every immersive/metaphorical element is **anchored to real evidence** — magic + artifact (real
  design work in the Workshop; real agents referenced near the Living Swarm; the real reel in the
  Story Fire). Magic *without* evidence reads as a fantasy art project; magic *with* evidence
  reads as a creative technologist.
- An abstract beautiful clearing with no link to real work is **incomplete**, not finished.

## §7 · State as narrative (dormant → alive)
- A space's **state** tells a story: dormant = potential; alive = the Maker's capability
  realised through the visitor's act. Transformation is meaning, not decoration.
- Progressive aliveness across a zone (the World brightening as the visitor advances) narrates
  momentum and the visitor's growing role.

## §8 · Composition as narrative
- Arrangement *means* something: order/care = the Maker's values; a tended path = welcome;
  a focal landmark = importance; a threshold = transition in the life-story (NARRATIVE_SPINE §4).
- Leading lines and framing don't just guide the eye — they tell the visitor *where the story
  goes next.*

## §9 · Forbidden storytelling moves
- ❌ **Boards/plaques/signs as the primary skill-or-meaning carrier** (museum/résumé failure).
- ❌ **Logo walls / tool inventories** as meaning (collector failure).
- ❌ **A literal Maker avatar** (breaks the Absent-Maker conceit).
- ❌ **Pure-metaphor clearings with no evidence anchor** (art-project failure).
- ❌ **Decay/ruin/abandonment** as the mood (the World is freshly tended, not dead).
- ❌ **Filler with no meaning** (dilution).
- ❌ **Text doing the job the space should do** (outside the Mountains' verification facts).

## §10 · What BELONGS / what does NOT
**Belongs:** a tended path with a lit lantern; a workbench mid-use with real work pinned; a
machine that springs to life when acted on; a clearing whose arrangement *is* its meaning; real
artifacts woven into metaphor; a space that changes state to tell a story; legible facts —
*only* in the Mountains.

**Does NOT belong:** a sign explaining a skill; a wall of logos; a hologram exhibit; a beautiful
but evidence-free metaphor; a ruined/abandoned mood; a depicted Maker; pretty filler that means
nothing; text substituting for spatial meaning.

## §11 · Evaluation checklist (for agents — "does this environment tell the right story?")
1. **Show-don't-tell** — meaning carried by space, not labels? (Mountains-facts excepted) (§2)
2. **Absent maker** — presence via traces, freshly-inhabited, no avatar? (§3)
3. **Earns meaning** — serves meaning/navigation/composition, not filler? (§5)
4. **Redundancy** — reinforces a *few* core ideas, not many thin ones? (§5)
5. **Evidence anchor** — immersive/metaphor elements tied to real work? (§6)
6. **State/narrative** — does state (dormant/alive) or arrangement carry story? (§7,§8)
7. **No forbidden moves** — none of §9 present? (§9)
8. **Tone** — freshly tended and humane, not ruined or melancholic? (§3)
