## §1 · World Overview (one page)

**What it is:** a cinematic, playable 3D world that is itself the proof of its claim — *the Maker
designs and builds intelligent experiences, and you are inside one.*

**Core purpose:** make a time-poor, skeptical recruiter *like* the Maker, *believe* in the work,
and *act* — while demonstrating the exact creative-technologist skill the portfolio claims.

**Core narrative:** a coming-of-age — *a designer who became a builder.* The visitor travels from
the world that shaped him, to the person himself, across the leap into craft, through the
capabilities he grew, up the record of where he applied them, into the proof of what he built,
and out toward what's next.

**Core recruiter journey:** arrive skeptical → **like** (Village) → **connect** (House) →
**reframed** (Forest) → **verify** (Mountains) → **convinced** (Projects) → **converted**
(Future). A rushed recruiter takes the **Fast Path** straight to proof.

**The altitude metaphor that organizes the whole World:** a **valley-to-peak ascent.** The
Village sits warm in a valley by a river; the Forest climbs gently beyond the bridge; the
Mountains rise at the far end; the Summit is the present; the Future lies beyond the highest
peaks. **Altitude = progression and time;** warmth cools to seriousness as you climb. Projects
are not on this line — they are pocket-worlds reachable via hub-and-spoke.

## §3 · Physical World Layout (sightlines, visibility, orientation, navigation)

The World reads as one continuous valley-to-peak landscape, so the visitor is **never
disoriented** and can always see *where they came from* and *where they're going.*

**What the visitor sees first (arrival):** dropping through the Portal into the Village valley at
golden hour. The **Hero House** is immediately visible as the warm-lit heart — instant
orientation. In the **far distance**, the **Mountains** rise as an aspirational backdrop, and
across the **river**, the **Forest** edge is visible beyond the **Bridge.** First read: *a warm
home, a wild beyond, a far summit to reach.*

**Sightline rules (canonical answers):**
- **Can the Mountains be seen from the Village?** **Yes — as a distant backdrop.** They establish
  the ultimate destination early (foreshadowing + orientation), but are not directly reachable
  (you must pass through the Forest). They are *aspirational horizon*, not *next step.*
- **Can the Village be seen from the Mountains?** **Yes — from the Summit**, looking back: the
  whole journey laid out below (Village, Forest, the camps climbed). This is the Summit's payoff
  (NARRATIVE_SPINE §5).
- **What is visible from the Overlook (Forest)?** The **Mountains**, revealed clearly for the
  first time (the Forest→Mountains handoff), *and* a look back over the Forest clearings resolving
  into one connected system. The Overlook is the World's pivot view.
- **What is visible from the Summit?** **Everything below** (Village, Forest, the climbed camps =
  the whole past) *and* the **Future** above (higher peaks in the dawn). Past and future meet at
  the present. This is also where the **Project Zero meta-reveal** lands.
- **What guides movement (visual landmarks):** the **Hero House** (the hub anchor), warm
  **lantern-lit paths** (human-centered wayfinding), the **Bridge** (the obvious crossing), the
  **river** (a natural leading line toward the bridge), the **distant Mountains** (the pull
  forward), and **light itself** (the brightest warm pool marks where to go — `LIGHTING §2`).

**Orientation logic:** the Hero House orients you in the Village; the river+bridge orient you
toward the Forest; the distant peaks orient the whole journey's direction; altitude tells you how
far along you are. You always know up=progress, back=origin.

**Navigation logic:** **narrative is linear, access is hub-and-spoke** (`NARRATIVE_SPINE §6`).
The Village is the wheel-hub; paths radiate to House, Bridge/Forest, and (via the **Fast Path**) to
Projects. The intended *story* order is the valley→peak walk; the *access* model lets anyone jump.

**Where Projects sit spatially:** Projects are **pocket-worlds entered via gateways**, not a fixed
point on the altitude line — reachable from the **Village hub** (Fast Path's primary destination)
and cross-linked from the **Mountain Gates** ("built here"). This honors hub-and-spoke and the
"small worlds you enter" model.

## §4 · Narrative Flow (the four paths through the World)

- **Story Path (the immersive visitor):** Space → Portal → Village → House → Bridge → Forest
  (all five clearings) → Overlook → Mountains (gates) → Summit (Project Zero reveal) → Future.
  The full coming-of-age, in order. Rewards exploration; longest, richest.
- **Fast Path (the time-poor recruiter):** Portal → Village → **direct gateway to Projects** →
  (optional) Future/contact. Reaches the proof in seconds; never detained. Always available.
- **Explorer Path (the curious):** free hub-and-spoke wandering — dips into functional places,
  the Market, Forest clearings, individual Mountain gates in any order; collects discoveries.
- **Recruiter Path (the evaluator's likely real route):** a hybrid — a little Village (likeability)
  → House (connection) → skim Forest (reframe) → **jump to Projects** (proof) → maybe Mountains to
  verify → Future (contact). The World must serve this impatient hybrid gracefully.

## §8 · Build Order (prototype → final)

- **Phase 0 — Exists (operational):** Village partial (bridge, river, market, farm, back lanes,
  hero house shell, street houses, entrance), Forest road/fence/trees/terrain, Day/Night toggle,
  proximity-overlay system. *(Tracked in operational docs, not canon.)*
- **Phase 1 — Vertical slice / prototype:** build **the Living Swarm** end-to-end (the signature
  proof and the boldest test of the magic-in-nature thesis — `EXP_LIVING_SWARM`). Walk it, confirm
  the feeling. This validates the whole experiential language before scaling.
- **Phase 2 — The proof:** build **Jarvis** (interactive flagship — real, showable) and stand up
  the **Projects** hub access + Fast Path. The hiring zone must exist early.
- **Phase 3 — The Forest body:** the remaining clearings (Workshop, Waterworks, Overlook, Story
  Fire) + the Forest→Mountains handoff at the Overlook.
- **Phase 4 — The record:** the **Mountains** Hall of Gates + context chambers + Gate→Project
  portals + the **Summit** with the **Project Zero meta-reveal.**
- **Phase 5 — The frame:** finish the **Village** (functional places, the personal signature) and
  the **Hero House** interior (personal story).
- **Phase 6 — The bookends:** **Portal** (arrival ritual) and **Future** (CTA/contact).
- **Phase 7 — Polish:** lighting/audio passes, atmosphere, performance, day/night across all zones.

> Build the *spine of proof* early (Swarm → Jarvis → Projects), so the World can convince even
> before it's complete. Never let documentation or framing zones outrun the proof.
