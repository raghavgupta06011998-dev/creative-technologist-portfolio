### D-001 · Identity locked: Creative Technologist
**LOCKED.** The Maker's identity is *"I design and build intelligent tools, agents, and
experiences that make work smarter"* — Creative Technologist (the union of Creative + Technologist).
**Rationale:** the project audit proved a genuine builder (production AI agents, a hand-engineered
voice workspace, this 3D world), not a designer who dabbles. Build is the engine; design is the
differentiator. *Supersedes:* the earlier vaguer "designer + AI" framing.

### D-002 · Forest concept locked: "The Maker's Forest" (self-portrait, 5 experiences)
**LOCKED** (`02-zones/MASTER_FOREST.md`). Five dormant→alive clearings performed by the visitor:
Workshop·REVEAL, Living Swarm·DIRECT, Waterworks·CONNECT, Overlook·SEE, Story Fire·KINDLE.
**Rationale:** capabilities should be *performed*, not read. *Supersedes:* six rejected Forest
directions — skill boards, glowing emblems, rock/monolith monuments, glowing cone "skill trees",
floating logo stations, and pillar monuments. All rejected for being label/museum/collector-style
or aesthetically off (cartoon/sci-fi). The root cause of the churn — *form chosen before meaning*
— became the law **"meaning before form"** (`BIBLE §5`).

### D-003 · Living Swarm grounded in nature (not sci-fi)
**LOCKED** (`03-experiences/forest/EXP_LIVING_SWARM.md`). The AI/orchestration centerpiece is a
warm, organic swarm of living lights the visitor DIRECTS — never a floating node network or
holographic UI. **Rationale:** "humane, not cold"; subtle magic integrated into nature.
*Supersedes:* the earlier "Constellation Orrery" (floating node network) concept.

### D-004 · Documentation architecture: the 5-layer Bible
**LOCKED.** `00-canon → 01-languages → 02-zones → 03-experiences → 04-evaluation` (+ `05-templates`,
masterplan docs at root). **Rationale:** single-source-of-truth via inheritance; uniform schemas
make automated agent evaluation possible.

### D-005 · Project Zero is a meta-reveal, not a Project World
**LOCKED** (`02-zones/MASTER_PROJECTS.md §17`). The "this whole world is one of my projects"
realization is revealed at the **Summit**; it also carries the design/experience-craft proof.
**Rationale:** as a peer room it was recursive and anticlimactic; as a meta-reveal it's a goosebumps
beat and removes the need for a third project world. *Supersedes:* the earlier "three Project
Worlds including Project Zero" assumption.

### D-006 · Projects = two worlds, cap three, no symmetry-filler
**LOCKED.** Jarvis (interactive flagship) + Agent Suite (impact story, simulated). A third only if
a future built artifact independently clears Tier-1. **Rationale:** memory over completeness; the
design-craft proof is carried by the Project Zero reveal, so no third room is needed.

### D-007 · Village = "where I come from / what shaped me" (given + self-built)
**LOCKED** (`02-zones/MASTER_VILLAGE.md`). The world that formed the Maker — inherited environment
*and* the environment he built for himself (self-learning, tools, side projects). Principles are
embodied as **functional places** (Workshop, Studio, Library, Market), never labelled.
**Rationale:** warmer, more personal, cleaner than abstract values; avoids the values-museum.
*Supersedes:* the earlier "Village = How I Think / abstract values" framing (and reconciled in
`NARRATIVE_SPINE §2–3`).

### D-008 · Village↔House wall
**LOCKED.** Village = the world that shaped me (external/nurture/communal — the soil). House = me
(internal/self/personal — the plant). **Rationale:** prevents the two most-similar zones from
blurring.

### D-009 · Mountains = "Hall of Gates" (Direction B, corrected)
**LOCKED** (`02-zones/MASTER_MOUNTAINS.md`). A verification zone: a hall of company gates leading to
**context chambers** (role/scope/outcomes) that **portal out to Projects** — *context, not content.*
Company identity is visible/legible (the deliberate show-don't-tell exception). **Rationale:** a
verification zone needs random-access legibility, not immersion; the Forest already owns
transformation. *Supersedes:* Direction A (the continuous transformation-climb), rejected for
duplicating the Forest and harming verification usability.

### D-010 · Access model: narrative linear, access hub-and-spoke; Fast Path mandatory
**LOCKED** (`NARRATIVE_SPINE §6`). **Rationale:** the story has an order, but a time-poor recruiter
must reach the proof without forced traversal.

### D-011 · MASTER_FOREST relocated into the bible
**LOCKED.** Moved from repo root to `design-bible/02-zones/MASTER_FOREST.md`. **Rationale:** it
predated the architecture; now sits with its sibling zone masters.
