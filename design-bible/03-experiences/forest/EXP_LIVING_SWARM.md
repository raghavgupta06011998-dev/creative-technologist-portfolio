## §1 · Essence (one line)
**A swarm of warm living lights, scattered and waiting, that reads your intent and organises
itself to do the work — so you feel, in your hands, what it is to command an intelligence.**

## §2 · Purpose
To make a recruiter *experience orchestration* rather than read about it. The Maker builds AI
agents that coordinate to get work done; the Swarm lets the visitor **direct that coordination
with their own movement** and feel it obey. It is the clearest possible proof of "I command
intelligent systems" — delivered as sensation, not claim.

## §3 · Narrative role
The Forest's centerpiece and its peak of wonder (NARRATIVE_SPINE / MASTER_FOREST emotional arc:
*curiosity → **wonder** → respect → clarity → warmth*). Within the self-portrait, the Swarm is
the Maker's living intelligence — the part of his mind that organises and executes. It is the
moment the visitor stops thinking "designer" and feels "this person commands AI."

## §4 · Recruiter role
The **signature memory** — the one beat a recruiter recounts to someone else six months later
(Bible §4, §11). It also reframes them: a *non-technical* recruiter who would glaze at "I build
AI agents" instead *feels* orchestration and gets it. Its credibility is backstopped by the
touchable Jarvis and anchored to the real Agent Suite (§13).

## §5 · Emotional role
A two-beat emotion: **wonder, then quiet power.** First the awe of a living, intelligent swarm;
then the specific joy of *having a capable team that instantly gets your intent* — the feeling
of leadership without friction. The visitor should leave feeling *capable by association.*

## §6 · Symbolism
- The **many lights** = many agents / many units of intelligence.
- Their **coordination** = orchestration; the whole exceeding the parts.
- **Following the visitor's lead** = the Maker directing AI toward goals.
- **Carrying motes between points** = work being routed, sorted, completed (echoing the real
  agents: assignment, prioritisation, synthesis).
- **Warm, living, natural light** = humane technology (Bible: *humane, not cold*) — intelligence
  as something alive and warm, not cold machinery.

## §7 · What the Swarm physically IS
- A population of **small, warm points of light** — read as *fireflies / drifting embers /
  living luminescence / lantern-motes*, never as UI dots, pixels, or sci-fi particles.
- Each light is a **soft warm glow** (honey-amber to warm gold by day; see day/night §16–17),
  with a gentle falloff halo — *firefly warmth*, not a hard neon point.
- Light count: **enough to read as a swarm, not a galaxy** — a crowd you could almost count, so
  individual purposeful behaviour is legible. (Tens, not thousands. Legibility over spectacle.)
- They occupy a **defined clearing volume** — drifting at roughly head-to-canopy height so they
  meet the visitor's eyeline and can be walked among.
- Grounded in the Forest: real foliage, mossy stone, golden shafts around them
  (`VISUAL_LANGUAGE`, `MATERIAL_LANGUAGE`). The Swarm belongs to the woods, not to a void.

## §8 · How it behaves (the behavioural model)
Three states, always organic, never mechanical:

1. **Idle / waiting (before the visitor engages):** the lights drift in loose, slow,
   **flocking-style motion** (cohesion, separation, alignment — a boids *feel*, not a rigid
   formation). They cluster softly, drift apart, occasionally **pass a brighter mote between two
   lights** — small signs of purposeful, coordinated activity *waiting for direction.* They are
   clearly *alive and intelligent*, but **dormant** in the sense of unled (MASTER_FOREST:
   dormant → alive).
2. **Directed (the visitor leads):** the Swarm **reads the visitor's intent from their movement**
   (where they walk / face / approach) and **flows to act on it** — converging on the spot the
   visitor moves toward and *doing the work there* (igniting a dormant point — §9).
3. **Resolved (a task completes):** the lights that completed a task perform a small
   **synchronised flourish** (a brief bloom/pulse together) — the satisfaction of done work —
   then return to coordinated readiness for the next.

All motion is **weighted, eased, frame-rate-independent** (`INTERACTION_PHILOSOPHY §8`): the
Swarm has soft momentum, never teleports, never jitters.

## §9 · Interaction design (the DIRECT verb)
- **The dormant clearing:** around the clearing edges sit a few **dormant points** — naturally
  themed (an unlit lantern, a dim flower, a darkened hollow, a cold ember). They visibly *want
  completing*.
- **The act:** the visitor **moves toward a dormant point.** The Swarm reads this as intent,
  **rushes to it, and ignites it** — the point comes warmly alive. The visitor learns in one beat:
  *where I go, the intelligence follows and completes the task.* (Trivial, never frustrating —
  `INTERACTION_PHILOSOPHY §4`. There is no wrong move, no fail state, no puzzle.)
- **The loop:** the visitor leads the Swarm point to point; the clearing **progressively blooms**
  into coordinated light.
- **The reward:** when all points are lit, the full Swarm performs a **final synchronised
  flourish** and the clearing reaches its most alive, beautiful state — the visitor's leadership
  made visible (dormant → alive, MASTER_FOREST).
- **Generosity:** rewards are immediate and visible; the Swarm always responds; the visitor is
  always flattered, never tested.

## §10 · How it reacts to the player
- **Presence:** as the visitor enters, nearby lights **turn toward them** and the Swarm's
  attention subtly orients — it *notices* them (the Responsive World, `INTERACTION_PHILOSOPHY §5`).
- **Leading:** the Swarm follows the visitor's *direction of movement/approach* — anticipating,
  flowing ahead toward where they're heading.
- **Parting:** if the visitor walks *into* the Swarm, the lights **part gracefully** to let them
  pass, then reform — never colliding, never fleeing in fear; an *intelligent, willing* yielding.
- **Idleness:** if the visitor stops, the Swarm settles into patient, coordinated readiness —
  not anxious, not random; waiting like a capable team awaiting the next instruction.

## §11 · How it communicates intelligence, trust, and agency
The three signals the experience MUST land:
- **Intelligence** — through **coordination and purpose**: lights move *together* toward goals,
  hand work between each other, and resolve tasks. Emergent, organised behaviour reads as a mind,
  not a particle effect.
- **Trust** — through **willing, calm responsiveness**: it parts for the visitor, follows their
  lead, never flees or malfunctions. It behaves like something that *trusts and is trusted*. Warm
  light reinforces safety, not threat.
- **Agency (the visitor's)** — through **cause and effect**: the visitor moves, the Swarm acts,
  a task completes. The visitor is unmistakably *in command* — the intelligence amplifies their
  intent rather than acting on its own agenda.

## §12 · Environmental storytelling
- The clearing is **tended** (Forest canon: freshly inhabited, absent maker) — the dormant points
  are clearly *someone's unfinished work*, waiting. The visitor completes the Maker's work, and
  in doing so animates the Maker's intelligence (self-portrait, `ENVIRONMENTAL_STORYTELLING §3–4`).
- Meaning is carried by **state and behaviour**, not labels (show-don't-tell). No signage explains
  "this is AI"; the swarm's *conduct* says it.
- Dense, living Forest surrounds and backs the clearing so the Swarm is a *discovery* as the path
  bends (MASTER_FOREST composition).

## §13 · Evidence anchor (grounding in real AI / agent-building)
Per `ENVIRONMENTAL_STORYTELLING §6` (magic + artifact) and the `MASTER_PROJECTS` relationship:
- The Swarm's **tasks mirror the real agents' work** — routing, sorting, prioritising,
  illuminating, summarising. The *behaviour* is the bridge from metaphor to reality.
- A **light, optional overlay-on-approach** (`INTERACTION_PHILOSOPHY §6`, diegetic-first, UI as
  restraint) names the anchor once — e.g. a single restrained line connecting the Swarm to *the
  real agents the Maker built (Glean, Rovo, and the Agent Suite)* — and/or a **portal/pathway to
  the Agent Suite Project World.** The metaphor is felt first; the evidence confirms it second.
- This anchoring is what keeps the Swarm *creative-technologist proof* rather than *fantasy*.

## §14 · How it avoids feeling like fantasy magic
Hard discipline (this is the experience's biggest risk):
- **Warm, natural light only** — firefly/ember/lanternlight. **Never neon, never cold blue,
  never sci-fi cyan** (`LIGHTING §4`, `VISUAL §5`).
- **Organic, weighted motion** — flocking/living, never geometric formations, never UI-grid
  snapping, never robotic.
- **No literal AI iconography** — no holographic neural nets, no code-rain, no circuitry, no HUD,
  no glowing wireframes. The intelligence is shown by *behaviour*, not symbols.
- **Grounded in the Forest** — real foliage/stone/light around it; it is a natural phenomenon of
  this world, not an effect floating in a void.
- **Anchored to real work** (§13) — the tether to reality is what separates "magic" from
  "metaphor for something I genuinely build."
- **Restraint** — fewer lights, legible behaviour, subtle glow. Spectacle is the enemy of meaning.

## §15 · User journey / discovery sequence / moment-to-moment
1. **Approach (anticipation):** the path dims; a warm flicker is glimpsed through the trees.
2. **Reveal (wonder):** the clearing opens — dozens of warm lights drifting with evident purpose;
   the visitor stops, taking it in.
3. **Notice (connection):** nearby lights turn toward the visitor; the Swarm acknowledges them.
4. **First act (discovery):** the visitor drifts toward a dormant point; the Swarm rushes and
   ignites it. *Click* — "it followed me. I did that."
5. **Lead (empowerment):** the visitor leads point to point; the clearing blooms; agency builds.
6. **Walk among (trust):** the visitor moves into the Swarm; it parts and reforms — intimacy and
   trust.
7. **Resolve (quiet power):** the last point lit, the full Swarm flourishes in sync; the clearing
   reaches its most alive state.
8. **Anchor (belief):** the restrained overlay/portal connects the felt experience to the real
   agents — wonder becomes credibility.
9. **Depart (afterglow):** the visitor leaves having *commanded an intelligence*, carrying the
   memory toward the rest of the Forest.

## §16 · Day version
- Lights are **warm honey-amber motes** catching the golden-hour shafts; visible and lovely but
  *gentle* — they coexist with daylight rather than dominating.
- Behaviour fully legible; the clearing reads as a sunlit grove with living intelligence in it.
- Glow intensity **low** (per the Day/Night system, `LIGHTING §7`): present, not blinding.

## §17 · Night version
- The Swarm becomes the **dominant light of the clearing** — the brightest, warmest presence in
  the dark Forest; a beacon visible from the approaching path.
- Glow intensity **raised** (emissive, no new real lights — `LIGHTING §5/§7`); the parting,
  leading, and flourish read as luminous events in darkness.
- The most magical version — but still warm, still organic, still never neon.

## §18 · Visual behaviour
- Each light: soft warm core + gentle halo + subtle additive bloom; slight per-light **twinkle/
  breathing** so the Swarm feels alive at rest.
- **Carried motes** are slightly brighter, briefly, as they pass between lights — visible "work."
- **Task ignition**: a warm spread of light from the Swarm into the dormant point.
- **Flourish**: a brief synchronised brightening + gentle outward pulse, then settle.
- Colour stays within the **warm band**; per-light variation is in *warmth/brightness*, never
  into neon hues.

## §19 · Motion behaviour
- **Flocking feel** (cohesion / separation / alignment) for organic life; never rigid formation.
- **Soft momentum & easing** — weighted acceleration/deceleration; frame-rate-independent.
- **Intent-following**: the Swarm biases toward the visitor's movement/approach direction.
- **Parting**: smooth local avoidance around the visitor, then re-cohesion.
- **No teleporting, no jitter, no snapping.** Motion is always continuous and calm.
- Idle motion is slow and patient; directed motion is purposeful but never frantic.

## §20 · Audio behaviour
*(Audio language is not yet authored; these are the binding behavioural requirements.)*
- A **soft, warm ambient bed** for the Swarm at rest — a gentle living hum / faint shimmer /
  airy chime cluster. **Natural and organic**, never electronic beeps, never sci-fi UI sounds.
- **Reactive cues, subtle:** a soft swell as the Swarm gathers to act; a warm, satisfying chime/
  bloom on task completion; a fuller, harmonious resolve on the final flourish.
- Audio **reinforces warmth and intelligence**, never alarm or machinery. Mixed low; it
  accompanies, never dominates. Must degrade gracefully if muted (experience fully readable
  without sound).

## §21 · What BELONGS / what does NOT
**Belongs:** warm firefly/ember lights; organic flocking motion; intent-following and graceful
parting; dormant points that ignite; a synchronised resolve flourish; a restrained real-agent
anchor; day-subtle / night-dominant glow; warm organic ambient audio.

**Does NOT belong:** neon or cold-blue light; geometric/grid/robotic motion; holographic neural
nets, code-rain, circuitry, wireframes, or any literal AI iconography; HUD/UI dots; thousands of
particles (spectacle over legibility); a fail state, timer, or puzzle; the Swarm fleeing or
malfunctioning; electronic/sci-fi audio; text explaining "this is AI"; the Swarm floating in a
void disconnected from the Forest.

## §22 · Failure modes (and the rule each violates)
- **Reads as fairy magic** → not anchored to real work (§13) / too whimsical (§14).
- **Reads as sci-fi** → cold colour, geometric motion, AI iconography (§14).
- **Reads as random, not intelligent** → no coordination/purpose/work-passing (§8, §11).
- **Breaks the agency illusion** → fails to respond to the visitor reliably (§10) — the single
  most damaging failure.
- **Overwhelming** → too many lights / too much motion; legibility lost (§7, §14 restraint).
- **Confusing to engage** → the dormant points or the DIRECT verb aren't obvious (§9 / `INTERACTION §4`).
- **Anticlimactic** → no satisfying resolve; the bloom/flourish missing (§9).
- **Disbelieved** → metaphor never tied to reality; recruiter enjoys it but doesn't connect it to
  AI capability (§13).

## §23 · Scope protection
- ❌ No literal AI/tech imagery, ever (it would collapse the metaphor into cliché).
- ❌ No turning it into a real minigame (difficulty/fail/score) — it is a *felt* moment, not a
  challenge (`INTERACTION_PHILOSOPHY §4`).
- ❌ No second swarm or competing AI set-piece in the Forest — this is the singular AI moment
  (memory over completeness; MASTER_FOREST scope).
- ❌ No neon restyle, no sci-fi restyle, regardless of "cool factor."
- ❌ No text explanation as the primary carrier of meaning.
- ✅ Allowed: tuning light count, glow, motion, audio; refining the real-agent anchor as the
  Agent Suite content matures; polishing the day/night behaviour.

## §24 · Quality standards
- Clears every `/01-languages` bar (Visual, Lighting, Material/emissive, Interaction,
  Environmental Storytelling, Asset/procedural).
- **Procedural** (per `ASSET_LANGUAGE §5` — it moves/glows/reacts): instanced lights, shared
  emissive material, no per-light real lights, frame-rate-independent motion, performant at the
  required count.
- Motion and reaction feel **smooth, weighted, and alive** on first contact, on any device.
- The three signals (intelligence, trust, agency) are each **unmistakably legible** to a
  non-technical visitor within the first interaction.

## §25 · Success criteria (checkable)
1. The visitor **directs the Swarm and feels it obey** (agency lands).
2. The Swarm **reads as intelligent and coordinated**, not random (intelligence lands).
3. The visitor **feels wonder, then quiet power** (emotional arc lands).
4. It **never reads as neon, sci-fi, or fairy magic** (grounding holds).
5. The visitor **connects it to real AI/agent capability** via the anchor (belief lands).
6. It is the **moment they remember** afterward (signature memory — Bible §4).
7. It performs smoothly in **both day and night** with no added lights at night.

## §26 · How to use this document
**Builders (dev / animator / VFX / environment):** implement the three behavioural states (§8),
the DIRECT loop (§9), the player reactions (§10), and the day/night/visual/motion/audio behaviour
(§16–20), holding the anti-fantasy discipline (§14) and procedural performance rules (§24) as
hard constraints.
**AI agents (evaluation):** to judge any Swarm asset/behaviour/change — (1) does it land the
three signals (§11)? (2) does it violate the anti-fantasy rules (§14) or what-does-not-belong
(§21)? (3) does it hit the success criteria (§25) without tripping a failure mode (§22)? Any
neon/sci-fi/AI-iconography/random-motion/non-responsive result is an automatic rejection.
