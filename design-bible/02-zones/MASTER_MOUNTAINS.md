## §1 · What the Mountains OWN
**The professional record.** Where the Maker applied his capabilities — the real companies, roles,
scope, and outcomes that verify the journey. It owns **legitimacy, trajectory, and credibility**:
the answer to *"is this track record real, and does it rise?"*

## §2 · What the Mountains do NOT own
- **Capabilities / the designer→builder transformation** → the **Forest** ("what I can do"). The
  Mountains must **not** re-tell the becoming-a-builder story; they record *where work happened.*
- **The artifacts / the work itself** → **Projects** ("what I've built"). Gates **portal out** to
  Projects; they do not contain the work.
- **Who I am personally** → the **Hero House.**
- **Where I come from** → the **Village.**

> **The Mountains↔Projects wall (most re-merge-prone): CONTEXT, NOT CONTENT.** A gate's chamber
> *describes and links* (role, scope, outcomes → portal); a Project World *shows* the artifact. A
> chamber that displays the work in depth is stealing Projects' job.
>
> **The Mountains↔Forest wall:** the Forest owns *transformation*; the Mountains own *record*. A
> rising title (junior→senior) is record (Mountains); "I became a builder" is transformation
> (Forest). Keep them distinct even though they rhyme.

## §3 · Why it exists / why the visitor is here
A recruiter must *verify* the person before hiring. After the Forest reframes them ("this is a
builder"), the Mountains answer the inevitable check: *"prove the track record."* Without it, the
Maker is charming and capable but unproven in the world of real employers. It converts capability
into credibility.

## §4 · Recruiter takeaway
**"The track record is real and it rises."** A clear, fast-read trajectory across real companies,
with the ability to drill into any one and jump straight to the work built there.

## §5 · The balances (zone tuning)
A **verification zone** — tuned opposite to the Forest:
- **Story ↔ evidence → evidence-led.** Facts first; minimal narration.
- **Interaction ↔ observation → observation-led**, with light interaction (enter a gate; look
  back; portal out).
- **Immersion ↔ usability → usability-leaning, strongly.** Legibility and random-access beat
  atmosphere. This is checking mode.
- **Exploration ↔ efficiency → efficiency-leaning.** Jump to any gate; never a forced climb.

## §6 · Recruiter journey
- **Before:** reframed by the Forest, now in *checking* mode — "show me where you've done this."
- **While:** reads the trajectory at a glance from the hall; enters the gate(s) most relevant to
  them; drills to outcomes; portals to the work.
- **After:** credibility established; trajectory understood; ready to be *convinced* by Projects
  and *converted* by the Future.

## §7 · Emotional / narrative / experience journeys
- **Emotional:** witness → credibility → respect ("real, and rising").
- **Narrative:** Act III's record — *here is where the builder applied himself.*
- **Experience:** arrive at the hall → scan the gates (instant trajectory) → enter chosen
  gate(s) → read context → portal to Projects or ascend → Summit.

## §8 · Layout & landmarks
- **The Hall of Gates** — a great hall carved into the mountain (gravitas; "you have climbed to
  the record"). Holds one **Gate** per company chapter.
- **Chronological verticality** — gates arranged along a short **ascending gallery**, earliest
  lowest → latest highest, so *progression/seniority reads at a glance* — **without** forcing a
  linear climb (the visitor may approach any gate). A whisper of altitude=seniority; not Direction
  A's full ascent.
- **Each Gate** — legible verification: **company identity, role, duration** above the gate
  (the deliberate show-don't-tell exception — company names are *legitimacy*). Tasteful, diegetic
  (a carved marker / banner), not a glowing billboard.
- **Context Chamber** (behind each gate) — light interior: role, responsibility, scope, outcomes,
  lessons `[FUTURE CONTENT]`, plus **portals to the Project Worlds built in that period**
  `[FUTURE CONTENT — link map]`.
- **The Summit** — above the hall: the **present self**, maximum perspective (the whole journey —
  Village, Forest, climbed gates — visible below), the **Project Zero meta-reveal** (D-005), and
  the **forward gaze** to the Future (higher peaks in the dawn).

## §10 · Visitor flow
Arrive at the Hall → **scan the gates** (trajectory in seconds) → enter the relevant gate(s) →
read context → **portal to the Project World** or return → ascend to the **Summit** → Project Zero
reveal → gaze to the Future. **Random-access** throughout; the **Fast Path** can still bypass
straight to Projects.

## §11 · Relationships
- **Forest → Mountains:** handoff at the Forest's **Overlook**, which reveals the Mountains. Tone
  shifts warm-magical → serious-credible.
- **Mountains ↔ Projects:** Gates **portal out** to the Project Worlds built in each period —
  *context → artifact.* Never merged.
- **Mountains → Summit → Future:** the Summit (present self + Project Zero reveal) turns the gaze
  from the climbed record to the higher peaks ahead (the Future / CTA).
- **Village/House:** no content overlap; the Mountains assume the visitor already met the person.

## §12 · Expression rules (how the Mountains must communicate)
- **Verification first → legibility leads.** Company/role/duration are **explicit and readable**
  from the gallery. This is the **canonical exception** to show-don't-tell (`ENV §2`).
- **Felt context, environmental** — each chamber's *character* may reflect the chapter's nature,
  but the **facts are explicit** and the **work is linked, not shown.**
- **Tasteful, diegetic identity** — gate markers carry company identity without becoming neon
  billboards (`VISUAL §5`, `LIGHTING §4`).
- **Cooler, more serious mood** — the Mountains' deliberate tonal break from the warm lower world
  (`VISUAL §8`, `LIGHTING §6`); still semi-realistic and humane.
- **Efficiency** — random-access navigation; no friction for a fact-seeking recruiter.

## §13 · What BELONGS / what does NOT
**Belongs:** a carved hall of legible company gates in chronological ascent; context chambers with
role/scope/outcomes and portals to Projects; tasteful diegetic company identity; a cooler serious
mood; a Summit with maximum perspective, the Project Zero reveal, and a forward gaze; random-access
navigation; the Fast Path.

**Does NOT belong:** the designer→builder transformation narrative (Forest); the project work
itself shown in depth (Projects — link, don't duplicate); hidden/obscured company names (perverse
for verification); a forced linear climb; Forest-level abstraction/magic; neon billboard logos;
résumé text-walls; a dead-end summit with no forward gaze; career *responsibilities* invented to
fill space (mark `[FUTURE CONTENT]` instead).

## §14 · Scope protection
- ❌ No re-telling the transformation (Forest owns it).
- ❌ No duplicating Project content in chambers — **context, not content** (portal out).
- ❌ No hiding company identity; no neon billboards either.
- ❌ No forced linear climb; random-access is mandatory.
- ❌ No more than the locked chronology's gates without a real new chapter; no fabricated
  responsibilities.
- ❌ No Forest-style immersion that taxes verification speed.
- ✅ Allowed without reopening: filling `[FUTURE CONTENT]` (responsibilities, outcomes, Gate→Project
  links); tuning gate prominence by significance; chamber dressing; mood/asset polish.

## §15 · Quality standards
- Clears all `/01-languages` bars (with the verification legibility exception in §12).
- Trajectory is **readable in seconds** from the gallery (instant verification).
- Each gate's identity (company/role/duration) is **legible without entering.**
- Chambers are **light** (context, not content) and **portal cleanly** to Projects.
- The Summit delivers **maximum perspective** and lands the **Project Zero reveal** without
  anticlimax.
- Cooler/serious mood reads as *earned credibility*, not coldness (still humane).

## §16 · Success criteria (checkable)
1. A recruiter grasps the **trajectory** (companies, roles rising) within seconds.
2. They can **enter any gate directly** (random-access) and read its context fast.
3. Each gate **portals to the relevant work** without duplicating it.
4. The record reads as **real and rising** (credibility lands).
5. The Summit lands the **Project Zero meta-reveal** and turns the gaze **forward** to the Future.
6. The zone never re-tells the Forest's transformation or shows Projects' artifacts in depth.

## §17 · Pressure-test record (what was challenged and decided)
- **Direction A (continuous transformation-climb, companies as checkpoints) → REJECTED** (D-009):
  duplicated the Forest's transformation and harmed verification usability (forced linear order).
- **Hall of Gates (Direction B) → CONFIRMED**, *corrected* so gates lead to **context chambers
  that link out** to Projects (not chambers that contain the work).
- **Hiding/omitting company logos → REJECTED:** perverse for a verification zone; identity is
  legitimacy. Made the deliberate show-don't-tell exception (§12).
- **Equal-weight gates → REJECTED:** later/senior chapters may read larger/higher (progression).

## §20 · How to use this document
**Humans:** build a legible, random-access hall of company gates that portals to Projects and
ascends to a perspective Summit; hold the *context-not-content* and *verification-legibility* rules

project content, hidden company names, or a forced climb are automatic rejections.
