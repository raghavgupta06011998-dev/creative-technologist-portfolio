> **Owns the question:** *"Where does this person come from, and what shaped them?"*
>
> **Purpose:** The authority for every Village decision. The Village is the visitor's first major
> world and the hub that connects everything. This document locks what it owns, its landmarks and
> layout, its emotional and visitor flow, its relationships, and its boundaries — so a designer,
> environment artist, developer, or AI agent can build and evaluate it without re-opening the
> philosophy.

---

## §1 · What the Village OWNS
**The world that shaped the maker.** The environment, influences, communities, habits, and
formative experiences that created the person the visitor will meet in the House — **including
the environment the maker intentionally built around himself** (self-learning, experimentation,
technology and AI tools, side projects, teaching himself new skills).

It owns **roots, belonging, and the emotional on-ramp**: making the visitor *care about the
person* and *understand the soil* before meeting the self (House) or judging the work (later
zones). It is also the **navigation hub.**

## §2 · The dual nature (the defining nuance)
The Village is shaped by **two environments held in one place:**
- **The world the maker was given** — organic roots, community, the broader creative ecosystem
  (most visible in the **Market** and the village's lived-in, communal fabric).
- **The world the maker built for himself** — the autodidact's self-constructed environment of
  curiosity and self-teaching (most visible in the **Workshop, Studio, and Library** — where he
  tinkered, experimented, and taught himself with technology, tools, and side projects).

Together they say: ***"Shaped by a world I was given, and a world I made."*** This duality is
what makes the Village uniquely the maker's and not a generic nostalgic town — it shows a person
who *took charge of his own shaping.*

> **Guard:** the self-built dimension is **warm, curious, handcrafted autodidact energy** — a
> personally assembled, experimental world — **never futuristic, corporate, or a tech showroom.**
> Self-learning is shown through *evidence of a curious mind at work*, not through screens and
> sci-fi.

## §3 · What the Village does NOT own
- **The self / personal story** → the **Hero House** ("me," in my own voice). The Village frames
  the House; it does not tell the story.
- **Capabilities** → the **Forest** ("what I can do").
- **Professional record** → the **Mountains** ("where I've done it").
- **Built artifacts / proof** → **Projects** ("what I've built").
- **Abstract value statements** → nothing. Principles here are *embodied as functional places*,
  never labelled (no value-buildings, no signs naming virtues).

> **The Village↔House wall (the key seam):** Village = *the world that shaped me* (external,
> communal, the soil). House = *me* (internal, singular, the plant). The Village must never tip
> into telling the personal story — it builds the *context* that makes the story land.

## §4 · Why it exists, and why the visitor arrives here first
You understand a person by first seeing **where they come from.** Roots → self → capability →
record → proof is the natural order of understanding a human. The House (me) only lands deeply if
the visitor has first walked the world that formed me. Emotionally, the Village is the **warm
on-ramp** that turns an evaluator into a *guest* — lowering defenses and opening the emotional
channel that drives hiring. The visitor cannot care about the proof until they care about the
person; they cannot fully meet the person until they have seen the person's world.

## §5 · Visitor takeaway
After the Village, the visitor feels: ***"I know where this person comes from. I like this world.
I want to meet whoever lives at its heart."*** Warmth, belonging, and curiosity about the House —
plus a primed lens (craft, creativity, curiosity, community) for reading everything later.

## §6 · Emotional journey
Arrival/threshold (from the Portal) → **warmth and safety** ("this place is alive and cared
for") → **belonging** ("I'm welcome here") → **curiosity** ("what is this place, who made it?")
→ **pull toward the House** ("I want to meet the person at the center"). The danger beat to defeat
is the post-arrival *"...nice, now what?"* — light, a landmark (the House), and a clear path must
convert it into *"ooh — there"* within seconds.

## §7 · Narrative & experience journey
- **Narrative:** the opening chapter of the coming-of-age arc — *here is the world that made the
  maker.* It sets the soil for everything that grows after.
- **Experience:** arrive → orient (the Hero House visible as the heart) → wander a warm,
  handcrafted, alive world → feel the formative places → arrive at the House (first destination)
  → continue outward (bridge to Forest, and onward). Hub-and-spoke; the Fast Path is present
  from here for the time-poor.

## §8 · Layout & landmarks
The Village is a **wheel with the Hero House at its hub**, compact and warm, with legible paths
radiating to each destination. Every landmark carries meaning:

- **The Hero House — *me*.** The emotional center and *first destination*; the heart the Village
  is arranged around. Visible from arrival as the world's anchor. (Owned by `MASTER_HERO_HOUSE`;
  the Village frames and leads to it.)
- **The Market — *the creative world around me*.** Community, collaboration, exchange,
  inspiration, learning-from-others. Its quiet argument: *"I didn't grow alone."* A genuine
  differentiator (most portfolios are lone-genius). The "world I was given."
- **The functional places (§9)** — Workshop, Studio, Library — *formative principles embodied,
  never labelled.* The "world I built for myself."
- **The Paths — *the connections of my world*.** Human-centered wayfinding; they lead first to
  the House, then onward. Their clarity reflects an organized, welcoming world.
- **The Trees / nature — *organic growth and nurturing setting*** — the living soil; also a
  **continuity device** foreshadowing and growing into the Forest beyond the bridge.
- **The Water / river — *time, flow, source*** — life moving through the world; best used as the
  river the **Bridge** later crosses, tying the Village's source to the Forest's threshold.
- **The Bridge — *the leap from roots to craft*** (canon transition): leaving the world that
  shaped you for the wild of what you can do.
- **Lanterns & warmth — *welcome and care*** — the world lighting the visitor's way, anticipating
  them (human-centeredness embodied).
- **The Portal threshold — *arrival into my world*** — the hand-off from Space into the Village.

## §9 · The functional places (the embodied principles — LOCKED set)
Principles are **felt through the function of a place, never read from a sign.** Locked set —
**four**, plus the Hero House (memory over completeness; do not proliferate):

| Place | Embodies (felt, not labelled) | World |
|---|---|---|
| **Workshop** | Craft & making — hands-on building | Self-built |
| **Studio** | Creativity & expression | Self-built |
| **Library / reading space** | Curiosity & self-learning (the autodidact) | Self-built |
| **Market** | Community, collaboration, learning from others | Given |

- The **self-built** places (Workshop, Studio, Library) carry the autodidact dimension —
  evidence of a curious mind teaching itself: experiments in progress, gathered tools, varied
  materials, side projects mid-flight — warm and handcrafted, never a tech showroom (§2 guard).
- The **Market** carries the communal/given dimension — lively, social, where ideas meet.
- Each place is recognizable by **what it is and contains**, so the visitor *feels* craft /
  creativity / curiosity / community without a label.

## §10 · Visitor flow
- **Arrival:** the visitor enters from the Portal and immediately sees the **Hero House** as the
  warm-lit heart — instant orientation.
- **First destination:** the paths lead to the **House** (Space → Portal → Village → House →
  rest). Meeting the person is the Village's primary payoff.
- **Exploration:** the functional places and Market are discoverable around the route — warm
  detours that deepen the sense of the maker's world, never required.
- **Onward:** from the hub, paths lead to the Forest (via the Bridge) and beyond. **Access is
  hub-and-spoke** (NARRATIVE_SPINE §6) — the visitor may proceed in the intended order or jump.
- **Fast Path:** present from the Village so a time-poor recruiter can reach the proof (Projects)
  directly. The Village earns affection fast and **never detains.**

## §11 · Relationships
- **Hero House:** the Village's center and first destination; the Village exists partly to make
  the House meaningful (context → self). The wall in §3 governs the seam.
- **Forest:** the Village's nature/trees foreshadow the Forest; the **Bridge** (over the Village's
  river) is the threshold from *roots* to *craft.* Tone shifts from warm-communal to wild-magical.
- **Portal:** the Portal hands the visitor into the Village — the arrival into "my world."
- **Projects / Fast Path:** the Village hub provides direct access to Projects for the time-poor.
- **Mountains / Future:** reached onward through the journey; no content overlap with the Village.

## §12 · Expression rules (how the Village must communicate)
- **Show, don't tell** (`ENVIRONMENTAL_STORYTELLING`): principles embodied as functional places;
  **no labels, no value signs.** *(Functional wayfinding signage is allowed — it's navigation,
  not skill/value content.)*
- **Functional, not labelled:** a place's *function* implies its principle (a workshop IS craft).
- **Warm but not slow:** emotionally rich, compact, and welcoming; never a sprawling
  autobiography. Earn care quickly; release the visitor onward.
- **Communal, not lone-genius:** the Market and lived-in fabric show a person shaped *with and by
  others.*
- **Handcrafted autodidact, not futuristic:** the self-built dimension is curious and warm, never
  a corporate/sci-fi tech display (§2 guard).
- All `/01-languages` bars apply (Visual: warm golden-hour cozy; Lighting: full golden hour +
  lantern warmth; Material: warm wood/stone/glass; Interaction: responsive world + Fast Path;
  Asset: realistic, reused, no excluded assets).

## §13 · What BELONGS / what does NOT
**Belongs:** a warm, handcrafted, alive town centered on the Hero House; a lively Market; a
Workshop, Studio, and Library that *feel* like craft/creativity/curiosity through their function
and contents; evidence of self-teaching and experimentation (warm, not techy); guiding lanterns,
benches, welcoming thresholds; nature and a river that foreshadow the Forest; clear paths leading
to the House first; a present, subtle Fast Path; one personal signature detail that makes the
world unmistakably the maker's.

**Does NOT belong:** labeled principle/value buildings or virtue signs; skills displays, tool
logos, or capability demos (Forest); project showcases or built artifacts (Projects);
company/career/résumé/timeline content (Mountains); deep personal-story content (House);
futuristic, corporate, or sci-fi elements; a lone-genius framing; decorative filler with no
meaning; a sprawling, slow autobiography that detains the visitor.

## §14 · Scope protection
- ❌ No new top-level meaning beyond "the world that shaped me." The Village is not a values zone,
  a skills zone, a work zone, or a personal-story zone.
- ❌ No labeled principle-buildings — principles stay embodied as functional places.
- ❌ No proliferation of functional places beyond the locked set (§9) without retiring one.
- ❌ No futuristic/corporate/sci-fi dressing (the §2 guard is absolute).
- ❌ No content that belongs to House/Forest/Mountains/Projects.
- ❌ No pacing bloat — the Village stays compact and warm; the Fast Path always present.
- ✅ Allowed without reopening: dressing/tuning the functional places; adding warm atmospheric
  detail that serves roots/belonging; refining wayfinding; adding the personal signature detail;
  asset upgrades within the languages.

## §15 · Quality standards
- Clears every `/01-languages` bar (Visual, Lighting, Material, Interaction, Environmental
  Storytelling, Asset).
- The Hero House is **visible and obviously central** from arrival (instant orientation).
- Every place is **recognizable by function** in seconds — the embodied principle is legible
  without a label.
- The world feels **handcrafted, warm, and alive** within the first moments (the "someone with
  taste and heart made this" beat).
- Wayfinding is clear enough that the visitor is **never lost** and is gently pulled toward the
  House.
- The self-built dimension reads as **curious autodidact warmth**, never tech-showroom.

## §16 · Success criteria (checkable)
1. The visitor feels **"this is where this person comes from"** — roots and belonging land.
2. They **like and trust the maker before meeting them** (emotional on-ramp works).
3. They **feel craft, creativity, curiosity, and community** *without reading any label.*
4. They sense the **dual nature** — a world given *and* a world the maker built for himself.
5. They are **drawn to the House** as the first destination and orient instantly.
6. A rushed visitor can reach the **Fast Path to proof** without being detained.
7. Nothing in the Village is mistaken for the House/Forest/Mountains/Projects.

## §17 · UNRESOLVED (do not treat as canon)
1. **The personal signature detail** — the one specific-to-the-maker element that makes the world
   unmistakably his (to be chosen by the Maker).
2. **Project access specifics** — exact placement of the Village→Projects hub gateway; the
   Fast-Path form (shared with NARRATIVE_SPINE UNRESOLVED).
3. **Whether the Village carries light interactions** (presence-reactions are canon; any verb-level
   interaction is open).
4. **Self-portrait whole-world scope** (whether the absent-maker conceit extends here) — open.
5. **Deferred (not blockers):** the specific real influences/communities/dressing that populate
   the functional places.

## §18 · How to use this document
**Humans (designer / environment artist / developer):** build a compact, warm, hub-centered town
that embodies the four functional places (§9) and leads to the House, holding the dual-nature
guard (§2) and expression rules (§12) as hard constraints.
**AI agents (evaluation):** to judge any Village proposal — (1) does it serve "where I come from /
what shaped me" (§1) and not another zone's question (§3)? (2) is any principle *embodied via
function*, not *labelled* (§12)? (3) does it violate the §2 guard (futuristic/corporate) or §14
scope? (4) does it move toward the success criteria (§16)? A labeled value-building, a skills/work
display, a sci-fi element, or personal-story content is an automatic rejection.
