## §1 · The arc

A **coming-of-age**: *a designer who became a builder.*

Roots (Village/House) → the leap (Bridge) → the craft grown (Forest) → the professional record
(Mountains) → the proof of what was built (Projects) → the open future (Future). The visitor
travels not through *sections of a CV* but through *the life of a maker*, and arrives
understanding a person, not a list.

## §2 · Zone ownership map (each zone owns exactly ONE question)

| Zone | Owns the question | Owns the feeling | Expression mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Portal** | *"Am I entering somewhere real?"* | Anticipation | Cinematic threshold |
| **Village** | *"Where do I come from / what shaped me?"* | Warmth, belonging, trust | Embodied environment (atmospheric) |
| **Hero House** | *"Who is this person?"* | Intimacy | Personal, inward, first-person |
| **Forest** | *"What can this person do?"* | Wonder, respect | Performed / interactive |
| **Mountains** | *"Where have they done it, professionally?"* | Witness, credibility | Legible verification + light interaction |
| **Projects** | *"What have they actually built?"* | Conviction | Real artifacts + tight story |
| **Future** | *"What now — will I act?"* | Momentum → action | Invitation / call-to-action |

**The Law of Walls:** *if a concept lives in two zones, the wall is wrong.* The most
re-merge-prone walls, and the rules that hold them:

- **Village vs House:** Village = *the world that shaped me* (external/nurture — environment,
  community, and the spaces I built around myself, embodied as functional places). House = *me*
  (internal/self — the personal story, in my own voice). Village is the soil; House is the plant.
  Village is outward, communal, atmospheric; House is inward, singular, personal.
- **Forest vs Mountains:** Forest = *capabilities and how I became capable* (transformation
  lives HERE). Mountains = *where I applied them professionally* (verification). The Mountains
  must **not** re-tell the transformation story — that is the Forest's.
- **Mountains vs Projects:** Mountains give **context, not content** — role, scope, outcomes,
  and *portals out* to the work. Projects **show the artifact.** The moment a Mountain chamber
  starts showing the work in depth instead of linking to it, it is stealing Projects' job.
- **Mountains vs Future:** Mountains = past→present (the record). Future = present→ahead
  (ambition + CTA). One vertical metaphor, split at the summit.

## §3 · Each zone: meaning, ownership, emotion, expression, and what it must NOT do

### PORTAL *(concept-level — see UNRESOLVED §9)*
- **Meaning:** the threshold ritual; crossing from the ordinary web into a built world.
- **Emotion:** anticipation, a held breath.
- **Expression:** brief, elegant, cinematic. Seasoning, not the meal.
- **Must NOT:** overstay, over-invest, or become a sci-fi spectacle that breaks the humane tone.

### VILLAGE — *Where I Come From / What Shaped Me*
- **Meaning:** the world that shaped the maker — the environment, influences, communities, and
  habits that formed him, **including the environment he intentionally built around himself**
  (self-learning, experimentation, technology/AI tools, side projects). Formative principles are
  embodied as **functional places** (workshop, studio, library, market), never labelled. Also
  the navigation hub.
- **Emotion:** warmth, belonging, trust — "this is where this person comes from."
- **Expression:** atmospheric and embodied; communal and warm. Principles *felt* through the
  function of places, never read from signs. Emotionally rich but never slow.
- **Must NOT:** tell the personal story (House's job), showcase capabilities (Forest's job),
  display work/companies (Projects/Mountains), or label principles as value-buildings. Every
  place earns its role by embodying a formative force, aiding navigation, or creating a
  memorable moment.

### HERO HOUSE — *Who I Am*
- **Meaning:** the emotional core; the maker's story and the designer→builder journey, in his
  own voice. The heart of the world, visible from arrival.
- **Emotion:** intimacy, connection — the emotional origin of the hire decision.
- **Expression:** personal, inward, first-person, a little vulnerable.
- **Must NOT:** become a values lecture (Village) or a work gallery (Projects).

### FOREST — *What I Can Do* · **LOCKED** (`MASTER_FOREST.md`)
- **Meaning:** "The Maker's Forest" — a self-portrait. Capabilities *performed* by the visitor
  across five dormant→alive clearings (Workshop·REVEAL, Living Swarm·DIRECT, Waterworks·CONNECT,
  Overlook·SEE, Story Fire·KINDLE). Reframes "designer" → "designer who builds intelligent
  experiences." Owns the transformation narrative.
- **Emotion:** curiosity → wonder → respect → clarity → warmth.
- **Expression:** performed/interactive; pure show-don't-tell; no boards; magic anchored to
  real evidence.
- **Must NOT:** become a skills list, a logo wall, or a museum. (Full scope protection in
  `MASTER_FOREST.md`.)

### MOUNTAINS — *Where I've Done It* · **Direction: corrected "Hall of Gates"**
- **Meaning:** the maker's professional record and credibility. A great hall carved into the
  peak, holding **gates** — one per company chapter. Each gate is *legible verification*
  (company identity, role, duration). Entering a gate reveals a **context chamber**: role,
  responsibility, scope, outcomes, lessons — and **portals out to the relevant Project Worlds.**
- **Emotion:** witness, credibility, "this track record is real."
- **Expression:** **verification zone** — legibility leads; this is the *deliberate exception*
  to show-don't-tell. Company identity is **visible and tasteful** (a gate marker), because for
  verification, company names are *legitimacy*, not clutter. Light interaction; the navigation
  is random-access (jump to any gate), not a forced sequence.
- **Must NOT:** re-tell the Forest's transformation; contain the project work itself (it gives
  **context, not content**); hide company names; become Forest-level abstract; force a linear
  climb. *(Continuous-climb / transformation-primary model was evaluated and REJECTED — it
  duplicated the Forest and harmed verification usability.)*

### PROJECTS — *What I've Built* · **the hiring zone**
- **Meaning:** the proof. Three worlds, each proving a distinct facet of the one identity:
  **Jarvis** (interactive flagship — touchable, hand-engineered, premium), **The Agent Suite**
  (impact story — production AI agents, shown as guided simulation + outcomes), **Project Zero**
  (this 3D world itself — meta-proof).
- **Emotion:** conviction; "this person is real and I want to talk to them."
- **Expression:** story-led (~60%), evidence-anchored (~40%): problem → insight → what I built →
  outcome. One deeply interactive flagship; supporting worlds lighter.
- **Must NOT:** become a Behance grid or case-study walls of text; dilute into too many
  projects (three featured; memory over completeness); absorb career history (Mountains' job).

### FUTURE — *The Invitation*
- **Meaning:** what lies beyond the summit — ambition, the next climb, and the call-to-action
  that converts a moved recruiter into a message.
- **Emotion:** momentum, hope, an open hand.
- **Expression:** forward-looking; higher peaks in the dawn; the contact moment.
- **Must NOT:** re-list the past (Mountains) or feel like a dead-end summit.

## §4 · Transitions / thresholds and their meaning

Transitions are *beats*, not dead corridors. Each carries meaning:

- **Portal → Village:** ordinary → built world. Crossing into the experience.
- **Village → House (inward):** the public worldview → the private heart. Going *into* the
  person.
- **The Bridge (Village → Forest):** the leap. Cultivated/known (roots, past) → wild/present
  (craft). Symbolically: *designing → building.* The single most narratively-loaded transition;
  treat it as sacred.
- **The Overlook (Forest → Mountains):** the Forest's fifth clearing literally reveals the
  mountains on the horizon, providing the handoff from *capability* (warm, magical) to *record*
  (serious, expansive). Tone shifts from intimate-magical to earned-credible.
- **Summit gaze (Mountains → Future):** turning from looking *back* over the climb (the record)
  to looking *up* at higher peaks (ambition). Past→present becomes present→ahead.
- **Hub spokes (Village → anywhere):** the Village is the wheel-hub; its radiating paths each
  preview their destination's meaning (intimate path → House; bridge → Forest; ascent →
  Mountains; a prominent path → Projects).

## §5 · Landmarks and their meaning

Each landmark is an *anchor of meaning*, not decoration:

- **The Hero House** — the heart; the human center; visible from arrival as the world's anchor.
- **The lit lantern over the forest path** — the first sign the wild is *tended*; "someone is
  here."
- **The Bridge** — the leap from designing to building.
- **The Living Swarm** — the signature moment; intelligence the visitor commands; the memory
  they keep.
- **The Overlook & its telescope** — insight; the moment the separate clearings resolve into
  one system, and the future (mountains) appears.
- **The Story Fire** — warmth; the maker's real work told as living shadow.
- **The Mountain Hall & its Gates** — the professional record; legitimacy carved into the peak.
- **The Summit** — the present self; maximum perspective; the whole journey visible below.
- **The Peaks Beyond** — the future; the next, unclimbed ascent.

## §6 · Linear narrative vs. hub-and-spoke access (the rule)

**Narrative is linear; access is not.** The *story* has an order (Portal→…→Future) and rewards
a visitor who walks it. But a recruiter is time-poor: **access must be hub-and-spoke.** From the
Village hub, a visitor can reach **Projects directly** (and ideally any zone) without traversing
everything. The **fast path** (a persistent, subtle affordance) lets a rushed visitor jump
straight to the proof. *Forcing* linear traversal is forbidden — it taxes the exact people most
able to hire. Reward the wanderer; never trap the hurried.

## §7 · The throughlines (woven across all zones)

- **The self-portrait / absent maker:** the maker is felt everywhere and seen nowhere; the world
  is his mind, walked by the visitor. *(LOCKED for the Forest; whole-world scope is UNRESOLVED,
  §9.)*
- **Friction-removal:** the maker's obsession — making work smarter by removing friction —
  appears at consumer scale (Jarvis), enterprise scale (Agents), and as the *responsive world*
  itself. This is what unifies the projects into one story rather than a list.
- **The responsive world:** the environment reacts to the visitor's presence throughout —
  continuous proof that the maker "builds experiences that consider the human."

---

## §8 · Cross-zone relationships

- **Forest → Mountains:** the Forest shows the *capabilities*; the Mountains show *where they
  were forged.* Handoff via the Overlook.
- **Mountains → Projects:** each company chamber **portals out** to the Project Worlds built in
  that period — *context pointing to artifact.* Never merged.
- **Village → Projects:** a prominent hub path provides **direct access** to Projects for the
  time-poor (the fast path's primary destination).
- **House → everything:** the personal story colours how the visitor reads every later zone.
- **Project Zero ↔ the whole world:** the portfolio names *itself* as a project; the Summit
  (present self) ties to "the thing I am building now."

## §10 · How to use this document

**Level/narrative designers:** build each zone to own its *one question* (§2) and to obey its
*must-NOT* list (§3). Treat transitions (§4) as designed beats.

**AI agents:** to evaluate whether a feature/asset/idea "belongs," locate the zone it touches in
§2–§3 and check: *does it serve that zone's single question, and does it violate any wall (§2
Law of Walls) or must-NOT (§3)?* A feature that belongs to another zone's question is
misplaced, however good it is.

LOCKED). Vocabulary used here is defined in `GLOSSARY.md`.
