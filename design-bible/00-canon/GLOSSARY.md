# GLOSSARY.md — Canonical Vocabulary

> **Layer:** 00 · Canon
> **Status:** LOCKED (living — terms added as the world grows)
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-31
> **Inherits from:** `PORTFOLIO_BIBLE.md`, `NARRATIVE_SPINE.md`
>
> **Purpose:** One canonical term per concept, so humans and AI agents share an exact
> vocabulary. Prevents synonym drift (the thing that makes automated evaluation unreliable).
> When any document or agent uses a capitalised canon term, *this* is its meaning. Each entry
> gives the definition, a usage note, and — where confusion is likely — a "not to be confused
> with."

---

## A. Top-level concepts

**The World** — The entire 3D portfolio experience, end to end. Preferred over "the site" or
"the app." The World is a *playable experience*, never a "website."

**Zone** — One of the seven canon regions: Portal, Village, Hero House, Forest, Mountains,
Projects, Future. Each Zone owns exactly one question (NARRATIVE_SPINE §2). No new top-level
Zones may be added (PORTFOLIO_BIBLE §12).

**The Maker** — The person the portfolio is about (its owner). In-world he is *felt but never
seen* (see Absent Maker). Use "the Maker" in canon docs rather than a name, so the docs stay
reusable.

**Creative Technologist** — The Maker's identity/umbrella: a maker who unites the *Creative*
(design taste, craft, human-centeredness) and the *Technologist* (engineering, AI, automation).
The portfolio's job is to prove **both halves at once.** *Not to be confused with* "designer
who uses tools" or "engineer who designs" — the point is the genuine union.

**The One Sentence** — The Maker's identity in one line: *"I design and build intelligent tools,
agents, and experiences that make work smarter."* Everything inherits from it. Exact wording is
tunable (UNRESOLVED); the identity is canon.

**Proof-of-Claim** — The headline idea (PORTFOLIO_BIBLE §0): the portfolio *is* the thing it
claims the Maker can do. Every decision is judged by whether it strengthens the Proof-of-Claim.

## B. The three recruiter questions (zone ownership shorthand)

**What I Can Do** — The **Forest's** question. Capabilities + how the Maker became capable
(transformation). *Not* where he did it, *not* what artifact resulted.

**Where I've Done It** — The **Mountains'** question. Professional record/verification: company,
role, scope, outcomes. *Not* the artifacts themselves (those are Projects).

**What I've Built** — The **Projects'** question. The actual artifacts (Jarvis, Agent Suite,
Project Zero). *Not* career context (that is the Mountains).

> These three must never blur. The Law of Walls (NARRATIVE_SPINE §2) polices them.

## C. Narrative & experience terms

**Self-Portrait** — The conceit that the World is the Maker's mind/identity, walked by the
visitor. LOCKED for the Forest; whole-world scope is UNRESOLVED.

**Absent Maker** — The device by which the Maker is *felt but never depicted* (lit lanterns,
tended paths, traces of work). No literal avatar of the Maker is shown. Keeps the World a
discovery rather than a presentation.

**Threshold** — A meaningful transition between Zones (e.g., the Bridge, the Overlook handoff,
the Summit gaze). Thresholds are designed *beats*, never dead corridors (NARRATIVE_SPINE §4).

**Landmark** — A meaning-anchor within a Zone (Hero House, Bridge, Living Swarm, Overlook,
Story Fire, Mountain Hall, Summit, Peaks Beyond). Decoration that is not a Landmark must still
earn its place.

**Clearing** — A Forest sub-location holding one Experience (Workshop, Living Swarm, Waterworks,
Overlook, Story Fire). Forest-specific term.

**Experience** — A single, self-contained interactive moment that *performs* a capability
(esp. the five Forest clearings). *Not to be confused with* "the World" as a whole, which is
also loosely an experience; in canon docs "Experience" (capital E) means a discrete unit.

**Verb** — The single action the visitor performs at an Experience, which *is* the capability.
The five canon Forest verbs: **REVEAL** (Workshop/design), **DIRECT** (Living Swarm/AI
orchestration), **CONNECT** (Waterworks/systems), **SEE** (Overlook/insight), **KINDLE**
(Story Fire/creative production).

**Dormant → Alive** — The core experiential loop: a space begins inert and the visitor's one
act brings it to life. The recurring proof that the visitor (and by implication the Maker)
*animates* the world.

**Responsive World** — The baseline principle that the environment reacts to the visitor's
presence everywhere, demonstrating "the Maker builds experiences that consider the human."

## D. Project terms

**Project World** — One of the three featured Projects, presented as a small explorable space.

**Jarvis** — The **interactive flagship** Project World: a voice-powered browser workspace with
genuine algorithmic depth (fuzzy/phonetic matching, self-learning correction) and premium UI.
The Project the visitor can *touch.* Proves Creative + Technologist simultaneously; fully
showable.

**Agent Suite** — The **impact-story** Project World: the Maker's production AI agents (e.g. QC
assignment, ticket intelligence, training synthesis) that real teams depend on. Shown as a
**guided simulation + real outcomes**, because the live systems are behind corporate walls.

**Project Zero** — **This 3D World itself**, named explicitly inside the experience: *"you are
standing in one of my projects right now."* The meta-proof.

**Friction-Removal** — The Maker's unifying obsession across projects: making work smarter by
removing friction. Expressed at consumer scale (Jarvis), enterprise scale (Agent Suite), and as
the Responsive World. This throughline turns the projects into *one story*, not a list.

## E. Mountains terms

**Hall of Gates** — The canon Mountains structure: a great hall carved into the peak holding one
**Gate** per company chapter. Chosen over the rejected continuous-climb model for verification
usability (random-access navigation).

**Gate** — A single company's entry in the Hall: legible verification (company identity, role,
duration) leading to a Context Chamber.

**Context Chamber** — The light interior behind a Gate: role, responsibility, scope, outcomes,
lessons, and **portals out to Projects.** Holds **Context, not Content.**

**Context, not Content** — The rule that keeps the Mountains↔Projects wall standing: the
Mountains *describe and link to* the work (context); the Projects *show* the work (content). A
chamber that displays the work in depth is violating this rule.

**Verification Zone** — A Zone whose job is fact-checking (the Mountains). Verification Zones
*break* show-don't-tell on purpose: facts (company, role, duration) must be **legible**.
*Contrast:* Reframing Zone.

**Reframing Zone** — A Zone whose job is to change a perception (the Forest: "designer" →
"builder"). Reframing Zones obey show-don't-tell strictly. *Contrast:* Verification Zone.

## F. Design-law terms

**Meaning before Form** — The process law: never choose an object/asset/visual before locking
what it must communicate. Violating it caused six Forest redesigns.

**Show, Don't Tell** — Meaning is felt through environment/interaction; labels are the last
resort. Universal *except* in Verification Zones.

**Memory over Completeness** — Optimise for the one moment a recruiter remembers, not for
coverage. Curate ruthlessly.

**One Identity, Many Expressions** — Variety is evidence of one identity, never a list of many.
The discipline that kills Diffusion.

**Humane, not Cold** — The mandated tone: warm, golden-hour, handcrafted; the opposite of cold
techbro AI. The reconciliation of "warm village" and "AI edge."

**Evidence Anchor** — A piece of real work attached to an immersive/metaphorical element so it
stays legible and professional (e.g., real design work in the Workshop). "Magic + artifact."

**Fast Path** — A persistent, subtle affordance letting a time-poor visitor jump straight to the
proof (Projects) without traversing the World. Required by the access model. *Not to be confused
with* breaking the narrative — the narrative stays linear; access is non-linear.

**Diegetic** — Belonging believably to the world (a lit lantern, a carved gate marker) as
opposed to **Overlay/UI.** Canon prefers diegetic expression; UI is a fallback.

**Overlay** — Non-diegetic on-screen text/UI (e.g., the proximity description panel). Used
sparingly, for depth-on-approach and the Fast Path; never the primary cue.

**Diffusion** — The #1 failure mode: becoming "a bit of everything," memorable to no one,
slottable into no role. The enemy that "One Identity" and "Memory over Completeness" exist to
defeat.

**Scope Protection** — The explicit "what must NOT be added" lists that keep Zones focused and
prevent churn/bloat (PORTFOLIO_BIBLE §12; each MASTER doc has its own).

## G. Audience terms

**Recruiter** — Catch-all for the hiring decision-maker the World is built for. Time-poor,
skeptical on arrival.

**Recruiter Archetypes** — Hiring Manager, Design Director, Startup Founder, Product Leader
(PORTFOLIO_BIBLE §3). The **Startup Founder / founding-designer** read is the working primary
audience (UNRESOLVED until ratified).

**Checking Mode** — The recruiter's mindset in a Verification Zone: wants facts fast,
random-access. The reason the Mountains prioritise legibility and jump-to-any-gate navigation.

## H. Document-system terms

**Canon** — The authoritative, locked vision. Lives in `/00-canon` (this layer), `/01-languages`,
`/02-zones`, `/03-experiences`. *Contrast:* the Operational layer (current build state:
`PROJECT_FULL_CONTEXT.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, etc.), which is volatile.

**Language (doc type)** — A global design-system document in `/01-languages` (Visual, Material,
Lighting, Audio, Motion, Interaction, Environmental Storytelling, Asset, Performance, Wayfinding)
inherited by all Zones. Defined once; Zones note deviations only.

**Master (doc type)** — A Zone document in `/02-zones` (`MASTER_*.md`), one per Zone, all sharing
one schema. The Forest's (`MASTER_FOREST.md`) is the reference and is LOCKED.

**Experience Spec (doc type)** — A component document in `/03-experiences` specifying one
Experience (e.g., the Living Swarm).

**Status tags** — Every canon doc carries one: **LOCKED** (authoritative; change only on a
genuine strategic flaw), **DRAFT** (in progress; not yet authoritative), **BLOCKED** (cannot be
written until a named decision is made). Agents must respect these: never treat DRAFT/BLOCKED
content as canon.

**UNRESOLVED** — An explicitly flagged open decision the Maker must make. Agents must not assume
answers to UNRESOLVED items.
